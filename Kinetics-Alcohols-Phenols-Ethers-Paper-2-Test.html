Before you start

61 questions 61 marks 90 minutes no negative marking
  1. All 61 questions are multiple choice and carry one mark each. There is no negative marking, so an informed guess costs nothing.
  2. The clock starts when you press the button below and runs for 90 minutes. The paper is submitted automatically when the time is up.
  3. You may answer in any order, change an answer as often as you like, and click a chosen option again to clear it. Nothing is marked until you submit.
  4. Use the numbered grid under the clock to jump to a question. A filled square means you have answered it.
  5. After submission every question shows the correct answer and the full explanation, so the marked paper doubles as your review.
  6. Nothing is saved. You can retake the paper as often as you like — the questions and their order never change.
